If you choose face check-in, the setup takes under a minute. You look at the camera, then turn a little to the left, then to the right. Three short captures, and that's it. Amoinee keeps only a numeric template, never a photo, and if the picture is too dark or blurry, it simply asks you to try again. You can withdraw your consent any time, and the data is erased on the spot.
