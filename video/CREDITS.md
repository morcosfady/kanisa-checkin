- **App footage**: recorded from the Amoinee app in demo mode with made-up data (scripts/record.mjs).
- **Music**: an original ambient pad and soft bell notes generated by `scripts/music.mjs`. No third-party music, nothing to license. Replace `raw/audio/music.wav` with a licensed track if you want something richer, and write its source here.
